Privacy: We don't create accounts, and we don't use cookies or any analytics/tracking scripts. Your chat isn't saved to a database — conversation content lives only in the server's memory for your session (which expires after 30 minutes of inactivity) and in your browser tab; refreshing or closing the page clears it. We do keep a lightweight technical log per session (which internal tools ran, timing, success/failure) to monitor reliability — it does not include your message text, drug names, or the estimates shown to you. Your questions are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI, depending on configuration) to generate a response, along with the plan/drug details needed to answer — we don't send your IP address or browser identity to them; review the provider's own policies for how they handle API requests on their end. Our hosting provider's own server logs may briefly retain basic request metadata (like timestamps) outside of this application's control. This is a plain-language summary, not a legal document.
